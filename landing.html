Good
questions.

Do I have to train to join?

No. Table is a kitchen-only membership: weekly meals at member prices and a guest seat at supper now and then. Add sessions whenever you like.

Where is Mika?

The kitchen, studio and long table are on the Maashaven in Rotterdam-Zuid. Meals are delivered by electric van across Rotterdam on Tuesday and Friday.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes, any week before Sunday 20:00, from the app. Pausing keeps your founding price; cancelling releases it to the waitlist.

What if I have allergies or eat plant-based?

Every dish lists its allergens, and at least four of the sixteen dishes each week are plant-based. Your profile filters out what you can't eat.

When do I pay?

Nothing until 1 December. We'll send your founding invite first, and your membership starts when you accept it.
